# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

crypto news push ("the app", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app, how that information is used, and what is not collected.

## Overview

crypto news push is designed to work without user accounts or a centralized user-data backend. The app primarily acts as a client that fetches public crypto and technology news from third-party WebSocket and RSS sources and displays that information on the device.

## Information We Process

Depending on how the app is used, the app may process the following categories of information:

- news titles, summaries, timestamps, links, and feed metadata fetched from public sources
- parsed symbols or tags used for feed display and filtering behavior
- local app state needed to show recent items and deliver local notifications
- standard network metadata inherently required to contact external news providers, such as IP address at the network layer

## Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The app does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

- name
- email address
- phone number
- home address
- exact location
- payment information

## Third-Party Data Sources and Services

The app connects directly to third-party sources to retrieve public content. These services may receive limited technical information that is normally exposed during HTTP or WebSocket communication.

Examples include:

- BWEnews WebSocket and RSS endpoints
- public RSS feeds from media or technology publishers
- translation providers, if translation features are enabled in a fork or future version

Each third-party service operates under its own terms and privacy policies.

## How Information Is Used

Information processed by the app may be used to:

- fetch and display news items
- notify the user about newly received items
- maintain app functionality and stability
- troubleshoot technical issues
- improve reliability in future releases

## Local Storage

Recent feed items may be stored locally on the device in order to support feed history, display performance, and duplicate filtering.

## Notifications

The app may generate local notifications when new items are received. Notification content is created from the news data fetched by the app and displayed through the Android notification system.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not operate a hosted account system that shares user profiles or contact information with advertisers or brokers.

## Security

Reasonable steps are taken to keep private material out of the public repository. Release signing files, keystores, and private credentials are not included in the public GitHub project.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected in this repository or future app releases.
